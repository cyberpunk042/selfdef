                    AUTHORIZED USE ONLY
================================================================================

This system is for the use of authorized operators only. All activity is
monitored and recorded by the selfdef IPS. Unauthorized access is prohibited
and prosecutable under applicable law (CFAA 18 U.S.C. § 1030 + relevant local
jurisdiction statutes).

The selfdef stack actively logs and bans IPs based on auth-failure patterns
(see: fail2ban-bridge module). Brute-force attempts will result in immediate
IP-level blocking + persistent recidive escalation.
